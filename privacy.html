Privacy policy, Claude Google Accounts

Last updated 15 October 2026.

Who uses it

Only me, Hayden Banas, on Google accounts I own or use. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it can do

With my permission it searches, reads, labels and drafts in Gmail, and sends only when I approve each message. It reads and edits my Google Calendar events.

Where the data goes

It runs on my own computer. Mail and calendar data pass between Google and that computer. Anything I ask Claude to work with is sent to Anthropic to handle my request. Nothing is stored on a server I run, sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Sign-in keys stay in my computer's Keychain.

Google user data

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

I can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
